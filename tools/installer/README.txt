FullerScope Installer
=====================

Installs the FullerScope app on Android phones over a USB cable. Nothing needs to
be installed on this computer.

1. Double-click "FullerScope Installer.cmd".
2. Plug in the phone and unlock it. (First time on a phone: click "Phone not
   showing up?" in the window for the one-time USB debugging setup.)
3. When the phone shows as "Ready", click Install.

Installing over an existing copy keeps the recordings on the phone.

Contents
  FullerScope Installer.cmd   start here
  FullerScope Installer.ps1   the installer itself
  FullerScope.apk             the app
  platform-tools\             Google's adb, which talks to the phone
